Travel Tips

Simple tips to make your journey safe and affordable.

Save Money

  • 💸Book flights early and compare weekday prices.
  • 🎟️Use city transport passes for metro and buses.
  • 🍽️Eat at local cafes instead of tourist hotspots.

Safety Tips

  • 🛡️Keep digital and printed copies of important documents.
  • 📍Share your location and itinerary with family.
  • 🎒Use anti-theft bags in crowded places.

Best Time to Travel

  • 🌤️Check weather patterns before booking.
  • 📅Visit during shoulder seasons for better prices.
  • 🎉Look up local events and holidays in advance.
